How it's worked out

The other tables' last two rounds go the way that puts the most players level with you or ahead.

Every other match is played out and the player ahead wins it.

A tie on points goes against you, so it reads Tiebreakers. A win is 3 points and a draw 1.

Pairings are modelled, not the store's, so take it as a guide.
