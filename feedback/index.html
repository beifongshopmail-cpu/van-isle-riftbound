Feedback

If something is broken, an event is wrong or missing, or you want the app to do something it does not do yet, this is where to say so.

A free tool for the Riftbound scene on Vancouver Island. It is not a contact form for any store, including the one that maintains it. Anything about a particular shop's events, prices or stock is better taken to that shop directly.

Recently shipped

Some of what came out of feedback like this.

  • See how far a card's price has moved, over a day, a week, a month, a year or all time.
  • Card art on the counter and the trade calculator, with a full-screen view on tap.
  • Saved trades, kept at the numbers they were agreed at, with a receipt image you can send.
  • Percent-of-market rates per side, so the calculator can match how you actually trade.
  • Match history on the counter, with legends and who went first.

Everything sent here gets read. Most suggestions will not get built, and there is no reply unless you leave an email.
