Trade offers

An alert on this phone when a player on the Trade board sends you an offer or a counter. The same switch as in Trade board settings.

Share my legend

After an event you played, the legend you set on the Game page goes on the Tier list's Local view with your placing, once Riot's final standings are up. Only the legend and the placing show, never your name. It needs your Riot ID.
