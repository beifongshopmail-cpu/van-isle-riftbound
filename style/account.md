# RAVI style notes: Your picture and the account menus

Pattern: Own design (a shared part every page with the wordmark row carries)

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching account.js or a page's picture.

The account picture and its menus (account.js, app.css)
- Every page with the wordmark row, and the hub, carries your picture (.acpic, plain glass on every page, never the page's tint, as Back; 36 round) in the row's top right corner, 18 in, level with the badge; it scrolls away with the row. None on the Tracker. It shows a legend's art or your photo filling the circle (class acart, never art: the card pages style .art as card art); with neither, a filled person (every phone has an account).
- Its menus (.panel.acct) are built by account.js, never by a page; their heads keep 68 on the right for the picture. The root shows your name and @username; Profile shows the picture at 88. Saving keeps you in the menu you saved from.
- Picture opens on a 176 circle you drag to move and pinch to zoom, the gesture read across the menu's whole width (#acstage, data-pinch) (a card keeps its placing; a photo is placed, then cut to 256 square); under it a Printing track for the chosen card (a legend's own art, then its overnumbered printing; any other card, each of its printings; never a signature); then Upload a photo, then Or pick a card: a search (Search every card, the round X) over every card in cards/pics.js; with no search, one circle per legend, four across; with one, each matching printing as a circle with its name under it (12, muted), names that start with the search first, 40 at most with a count. A landscape battlefield is placed to fill the circle. A pick shows in the corner at once, is ringed in the grid, and is kept by Use this picture.
- A button that saves says so in its own words for a moment: Saved, Claimed.
- Profile holds Your colour, a .seg.swatch of the eight player colours kept by Save, previewed on Profile's picture before Save.
- Profile holds the Riot ID, one account per Riot ID, in two fields as Riot shows it, the name and the #tag (.rid), with Claim or Remove Riot ID as a full-width action under them; the root menu's second line reads @username and the Riot ID. An account in ADMIN also has Disputes (Give it to @user, Keep it where it is), Reports, and Notices: what the events refresh found (a town's first events, a town not on the island's list, a store with no short name; data/notices.json), a row with the count this phone has not opened, its menu listing them newest first with the unopened marked New in words.
- The root menu: an account never saved shows Save your account first, a note that only this phone reaches it, then Profile and Your data, then Sign in to another account; a saved one shows Profile, Link a new device, Sign-in and security (Passkeys, Devices, Recovery code, as Apple Account's), Your data (and Disputes, Reports), then Sign out alone. Sign in to another account opens Use a passkey and Another phone, then Recovery code under the label Lost all your devices?
- Wherever a page needs a saved account it shows a Save your account row (RAVI_SAVEROW in account.js), which opens the account menu; never directions such as 'tap your picture'. RAVI_ACCT_OPEN(menu) opens it at a deeper menu (the Riot ID's one home, Profile).
- Linking a phone: Another phone shows a six-letter code in two threes (.accode at 20) over its QR, dark on a white 176 plate (.aclkqr, corner 12, the code alone in it), one centred line on what to do, and how long it lasts; a code that runs out dims both and wakes New code in the foot. Link a new device is a field (Code on the new phone) with Scan as its .fbtn; Scan opens the camera in a 240 square (.aclkcam, corner 22) under the field and closes it once read; the note names the waiting phone; the foot's action reads Link this iPhone (or the phone's name) and stays dim until a code is found.
- Lists inside the account menus are rows: a passkey by its name with its last-used day (it opens a Passkey menu: Name, Remove this passkey, dim while it is the only one); another device by its name with its last-used day, a two-tap row that signs it out, and Sign out every other device under them once there are two. Days are the phone's own, never the server's UTC day. Download your data hands the file to the phone's share sheet where there is one.
- The first-time setup lives in these menus, opening from your picture: once, on the installed app's first hub as the intro ends (never behind the install screen or for a test tool; vir.setup.v1), and again from Guide's Set up RAVI. Five steps, each head counting it (.cnt, 1 of 5): Your account (Save your account, then the recovery code shown once), Riot ID (Claim), Your areas (each town with events as a pick-many row with its count, the same saved choice as Events settings' Areas), Alerts (Trade offers), Your hub (Hub layout); then All set, with a row to the bar's tour (Show me around; style/tour.md) and a row to Guide. Each foot reads Not now until its step is done, then Next; Back returns a step; closing it counts as done.

Page-local classes: none; account.js's classes live in app.css.
- Your data (plan 2 M9 block 10): under Download your data and Delete account, a press group of one deeper row, Privacy (#acpriv), to App settings' Privacy menu (settings/?open=pprivacy).
