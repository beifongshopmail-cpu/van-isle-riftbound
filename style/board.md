# RAVI style notes: Exchange

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Exchange (board/index.html; plan 2 M2, 2026-11-01)
- No lede: a sticky .plate.glass (the search, then Matches | Players | All cards), then the list head as Decks' list of decks (the count; Sort: Most in demand, Highest price, Name, a pick closes the menu; players near you always first). Matches: a glass card per player (picture, name, @username and area, Both ways as plain 12 muted words, never a pill), a shelf each way, yours first (Wants n cards you have, Has n cards you want; M7 B5 and B6, Mako's phone yes 2026-11-04); Players: a block per player with four cards; All cards: the Collection's grid. Most in demand is board/demand.js.
- Menus: #pwho (a player: Open to and their price, their cards at it, Yours then Theirs, Price this trade, Make an offer or See your offer), #pcard (who has it), #psort, #pset (Exchange settings: what you share, How others see you, #pself, Open to, My price with - and +). Bar: Hub, Go to, Offers (a bone count of offers waiting on you, said in its label too), Settings. Others see a card, its printing and count, with the player's name, @username, home area and picture; never a price paid, condition, rank or record; the Riot ID only to the other player once a trade is agreed.
- Offers (plan 2 M2 block 2; Mako's yes 2026-11-01): #poff lists Waiting on you, Agreed, Sent, Past as player rows; #poffer (deeper, titled by the player) shows the state in words, You give and You get grids (yours first, as #pmake's), the verdict, cash and Riot ID rows, Meet at (You opens #pmeet; Them a row), then its actions as rows (Accept, Counter, Decline; Withdraw; Trade done and Call it off, each two-tap but Accept and Counter). #pmake: tap a card to put it in, again for another copy, past its count to take it out (a bone ring and check, In: n of m); Cash as No cash | You add | They add with a dollars field; the verdict; Send offer in the foot, dim with the reason in words until it can send.
- The verdict in an offer is the Trade page's made small: Yours (left, the Appraise page's blue) and Theirs (right, its pink) with their values and counts, the difference between, an arrow at the side worth more or Even.
- Meet at (#pmeet): Not decided, then the stores with events in the next three weeks A to Z, each a two-line row (its count and next date) opening in place to its events, set in 16 more; every row a name with a line under it.
- A player's card on the board presses as a whole (scale .98), as Decks' deck cards.
- A player with a colour (plan 2 M8; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-04): their card (.bt) is coloured glass in it (.cg), their menus' head block (.btop) too (.btop.cg: padding 10 12, corner 22), and their picture (.bpic) wears it as its 1 wide rim (.accol) everywhere on the page. Without one, plain glass. An offer's verdict colours Yours and Theirs as the Appraise page does (--yc, --tc from RAVI_SIDES_PAINT).
- Report or block (plan 2 M2 block 3; Mako signed off on the phone 2026-11-01): a player's menu and an offer end with a Report or block row opening #prep: What's wrong? as five action rows (Their picture, Their name or username, Unwanted offers, A trade went wrong, Something else; dim for an unsaved account, a sent one shows Sent), then Block <name>, two-tap. No free text. Only RAVI sees reports; a block is silent and takes both players off each other's board.
- Blocked players sits in Exchange settings with How others see you, its count as the row's value; #pblk lists each as a player row with Unblock in place of the chevron.
- Matches with no match yet (Mako 2026-11-09: appealing while empty): the two halves of a match as two coloured glass tiles side by side (.bstart, .bside.cg: corner 22, padding 14), You want in your colour and You have in theirs (--ycr, --tcr), each saying where it stands (n cards on your Wishlist, n cards for trade, or what to do) and its way to Collection (13 with the row chevron), then one centred line (No matches yet, and what the Exchange does) and See all players (.pact.bsee), which switches to Players. A search with no hit keeps the plain line. As built, not chosen.
- With no home area, a Set your home area row (players near you first) opens Profile, never directions (plan 2 M8.7 A4). A player's menu writes Yours and Theirs in the sides' colours (.ny .nt, --yc --tc; M8.7 C6).
- A player taken off the board reads why in the line under the plate (and where to ask: Feedback); a hidden picture is said there too.
- Mako's Reports (account menu, beside Disputes, with a count): each reported player once, picture, name and @username, then a note (off the board, picture hidden, the reasons counted, who sent them), then their actions as two-tap rows: Remove picture, Clear name and username, Take off the board, Dismiss; Put back on the board once off.

- The line 'Matched against your Wishlist ... and your For trade binder.' shows on Matches only (plan 2 M8.7 A4, Mako's yes 2026-11-10).
