Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Calendar (calendar/index.html)
- Sticky month card (.stick zero-height holder, .card, .hold spacer, arithmetic height), .mrow with month, year, the .hemi pair and the collapse chevron (#mtog).
- Day cells .cell (circles, data-k grid), dots under them; agenda of .ev cards with .evhead and .evact: under the in-card line, the street address (.evaddr 13 bone, .evend 12 muted when the feed has an end), then under it one row of equal pills, 6 apart, each its icon over its word (.evpill, --sz-evpill 52, corner 20; .evlb Poppins 600 12, tracked -.06em under 390 so five stay whole at 360; Apple Maps' place card; M7 C1, Mako's phone yes 2026-11-04) (.evbtns: .evgo Going for a saved account, .evdir Directions to the phone's maps, .evadd one event's file data/ev/<id>.ics, .evcut the Top cut page for that event (../cut/?ev=<id>, in the app; every kind but Learn-to-Play and Open Play; M15), .evlink the event page), their words Going, Directions, Calendar, Top cut, Website, each with its aria-label; all but .evcut and .evgo open outside the page. No Play here; the counter's ?ev= link stays a hidden entry.
- Live (M16, 2026-10-27): for today's events near their start (every kind; from plan 2 M5 Open Play and Learn-to-Play too, shown only when the event runs rounds) live.js asks RAVI's server once a minute. A running event's card adds one line under its name, a bone LIVE pill and where it stands in words (Round 2 of 3, 39 min left); its open row adds a .pact, Pairings and standings (Final standings once it ends), that opens #plive "Live": the event's name (13 muted), the round and its clock (Over time past zero, in --over with the words), my table in a glass card (tap it to change my Riot name; no name saved: a field, Name#TAG, with Find me), Pairings | Standings, a round track, then read-only rows (no kind, no surface, hairlines): table number, both players with their tag in 12 muted, each result in words (Won 2-1, Lost, Draw, Playing), the winner in bold, You on mine; standings as #, Player, Pts, W-L-D, OMW. Foot: Alert me when I'm paired (dimmed with a line saying why until a name is saved; Alerts on: tap to stop), Open the event page. Server away: nothing live shows. ?live=<id> opens the menu (an alert's link). The test event (?sim=1) plays pretend live data from live.js (never the server) so every live part can be tried; its foot sends a test alert instead.
- Menus: tray #pnav; #panel "Calendar settings" (Areas .pick, Types .pick, Stores .pick; the Area track, Greater Victoria | Whole Island, was retired 2026-11-10, Mako); #psub "Follow"; #plive "Live". Bar action: Follow (#subtab, the bell, data-fills psub). The bar's Settings (#tabset) is the one way into #panel; while its type or store rows hide events, the list head's count says so ("15 events, 4 hidden"), over the same days and area the list shows.
- Follow (#psub): an (i) in the head opens the note in place (.finfo). One sunken group (#fall), each feed row a webcal link to a file the refresh writes (ics.js: island.ics, victoria.ics, upisland.ics, store/<id>.ics): Whole Island, then Greater Victoria and Up Island as open rows (kind open, a chevron) that list their feeds under them, one area at a time: All stores, then each store A to Z, set in 16 more (.fsub). A row tapped on this phone shows a bone bell (.fmk) left of its count (vir.follow.v1); RAVI cannot see the phone's calendar, so the bell means tapped, not followed. A row whose events are already in a feed tapped here (Whole Island over any area or store; an area's All stores over its stores and back; the row itself) asks first: it reads 'Already in <feed>' or 'Added before on this phone' with 'Tap again' where the count was, and only the second tap goes to the calendar app; the ask lapses after 4 s. (Mako's phone yes 2026-11-03)
- The month folds while the list scrolls (plan 2 M8.7 A3, as Fantastical): once the agenda's top passes under the card (#foldmark, an IntersectionObserver, never a scroll listener) the card folds to the chosen day's week, the chevron's own move; back at the top of the page it opens again; a tap on the chevron wins until then.
- Live's Riot name is two fields (.rid: Riot name, # tag) with Find me under them, as Profile (plan 2 M8.7 B8); with no name the foot's line reads 'Alerts need your Riot name.'
- A store's own post (plan 2 M8.7, Mako 2026-11-10; fetch-events.js STORE-POSTED EVENTS): an event read from a store's post and not on PlayRiftbound (src store) is a card as any other, of its kind, with a flat tag in words at the top right of the card, where the spots count would be (Mako 2026-11-10), Not on PlayRiftbound (.evsrc: 12, Nunito 600, bone on --flat, a plain pill; never a fill, never colour alone), and no spots count; under 390 wide the top line has no room, so the tag ends the venue line instead (two copies, CSS shows one). Opened: its end when the post gives one, then one line, 'From the store's own post. Check with the store before you go.' (.evpostnote, as .evend), then Going, Directions, Calendar and Post (.evlink.evpost, the out-arrow, the store's post, outside the page); no Top cut, no Website, no Pairings and standings. (Mako's phone yes 2026-11-10, the tag at the top right at his word)
- Areas (Mako 2026-11-10; the whole island is searched from that day): the only area choice; each area is a town. Events settings is three lists, Areas, Types, Stores (his words 14:52; the code and older notes say Towns and Event types): Areas lists each town that has events, A to Z, as a pick-many row with its count and check (.tyrow, the Kit's pick-many); an area turned off hides its events, counted in the list head's hidden; kept with the other filters (vir.cal.filters.v1, towns: the ones off). With nothing saved every area is on. A phone that had saved Greater Victoria has its Up Island towns turned off the next time Events opens. A store follows its area: while its area is off its row is unchecked and dim (disabled, holding its place); back on, the store is as it was left, so one turned off by hand stays off (no lock control). The first-time setup's third step, Your areas, sets the same choice (account.js). Follow's feeds stay by area and store. (Mako's phone yes 2026-11-10 14:52; the names and the stores following, his yes 17:32)
- NEW on a card means new since the last visit: badges stay while the page is open; leaving it (pagehide, or the page hidden) marks every listed event seen (vir.seen.v3).

- The Settings slot's menu is titled Events settings, the page's name (plan 2 M8.7 A5, Mako's yes 2026-11-10; code and older notes say Calendar settings).
