# RAVI style notes: Cards

Pattern: Browse (the reference page for Browse)

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Cards (cards/index.html; plan 2 M9 block 3, design agreed with Mako 2026-11-10 19:17, built 2026-11-10; round 2 on his phone check 2026-11-11: a card is a menu, Set and number sorts first, the colour a neutral)
- Every card in one place. A front end only: cards/catalogue.json (fetch-cards.js), data/prices.json and data/rules.json, read through decks/deck.js (the name, the printings, the ban list). The page's own index.html shares the folder with the card data. Prior art, patterns only: Scryfall and Piltover Archive (search first; a card page of text, printings with prices, legality, rulings).
- The page is search first. A sticky search plate (.srow .plate, as Rules' and News'): .sf ('Search cards') with the round X; under it (.dvrow) Sort and one Filters chip (.fchip, as Decks' browser: Sort shows its arrow and name; Filters counts what is on and fills bone). Grid | List (.seg.dvseg, the view chips' icons; kept in vir.cards.set.v1 with the sort) sits at the right of the list's head, after the count: 'Set and number' beside it would not fit the plate's row at 360. Sort (#psort, rows, the arrow on the chosen one, again flips it): Set and number (the default, Decks' browser's and Collection's words: the sets in release order, then the number), Name, Energy, Price. Filters (#pfls, rows with their values, each one deeper to a pick-many list #pfl with Any in its foot; Show all in its foot): Set, Type, Domain, Rarity, Energy. A card matches a Set or Rarity when any of its printings does, a Domain when it has one of the picked ones.
- Each card shows once, as its base printing (deck.js rank). A search word must be in the card's name, number, tags or type; cards that match only in their text follow those. The section head reads All cards, or Results, with the count. Grid is the Decks browser's grid, three across (.ctile): the picture is the tappable part (.cpic, kind card, a battlefield turned), the name and the price of one under it. List is rows without glass, as Collection's (.crow): picture, name, what it is, the price. The next 60 join as the end nears; Back to top (RAVI_TOTOP). Nothing matching says 'No card matches.' over Show all cards. Loading blocks: a head and nine tiles.
- A card opens as a menu (#pcard), as a card does everywhere else in RAVI (Decks', Collection's, the Exchange's card menu; Mako 2026-11-11: the first build's full-screen view felt unlike the rest of the app). It opens from the card tapped; the head is the card's name and Close; the foot is Add to. The body, in this order: the picture (.mpic, 150 wide as Decks' card menu; a battlefield landscape, 230), a tap shows the full card (.spot); Riot's text in a glass card (.ctxt, .rtx), keywords as their tags (RAVI_KW_TAGS) and Riot's :rb_ symbols as Riot's glyphs (.ksy); its numbers, a glass card of rows you read (.facts .fk: Type, Domain, Energy, Power, Might, Might bonus, Tags, Rarity, Number, Artist, Constructed: Legal or Banned, in words); Printings and 7-day price moves, a sunken list you pick in (.plist .prb, the check on the picked one; the picture, Rarity, Number and Artist follow the pick): the printing's picture, its set and kind, its number, and each finish's price with its 7-day move (.fin .mv: the arrow and the percent, never a colour); Errata and rulings, from Rules: one press group whose rows open their words in place (.door.rec.rul, kind open, .rbody), the kind's word in its Rules section's colour, lit (.rty: Errata garnet, FAQ and Ruling gold), an erratum showing New text then Old text.
- Which entries name a card: an erratum by Riot's own card list; a question or ruling when its words hold the card's whole name as written ('Ahri' alone is not 'Ahri, Alluring'; a legend also by its title).
- Add to (#padd, one menu deeper, Back in its head): a Finish track when the picked printing has more than one; Collection, one press group of action rows (Wishlist, No binder, each binder), each showing in grey how many it holds; Decks, each deck with how many it holds, or Build a deck (No decks yet) going to Decks. A tap adds one, the count moves and a toast says where ('Added to the Wishlist'); the menu stays open. Written as Collection and Decks write their own keys (vir.coll.v1, vir.decks.v1). A deck takes a Main deck card always, a legend only while it has none, a battlefield while it has fewer than three; runes and tokens are dim. A card with no price yet dims the Collection rows.
- Banned (Mako 2026-11-11): a banned card wears Decks' BANNED pill (.ban: red --over, the no-entry mark and the word, 22 high, 12 Poppins 700) centred near the foot of its Grid picture and of its picture in the card menu (.bstack, .bstack.menu, as Decks' grid and card menu place it), and at the right end of its List row over its price (.cend). Constructed still says Banned in words.
- Ways in: Decks' card menu, Collection's card menu and the Exchange's card menu each end with Open in Cards (.tocard, a .pact link), so nothing links out. ?c=<set-number> opens that printing's card, ?n=<name> a card by name (the price feed's names are read; one RAVI cannot place becomes the search). Reached that way the card's menu opens over the list, and closing it leaves the list.
- Bar: Hub, Go to, the third slot dimmed (Soon), Settings dimmed. Go to: Your cards, after Collection and Decks. Not on the hub's default seven. The footer is the price source line (RAVI_STAMP) alone (footers trimmed 2026-11-12, Mako's yes 20:56).
- Page colour: parchment 213,192,174 (Mako 2026-11-11: closer to what Decks uses, a white or a neutral, not the same). Body's own hue (H62, Collection's orange is H57) drained to a near-neutral, so the group reads orange, parchment, white; the farthest such neutral from every page colour and the players' (rb-tools design/colour/cards.py: .137 bare, .051 in the hub's glass). Clay 164,60,51 was the first build's (his pick over peach, 2026-11-10). Icon: a card under a magnifier, drawn for RAVI on Lucide's construction, provisional until the icon pass (plan 2 M9 block 9).
