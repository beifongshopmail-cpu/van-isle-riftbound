# RAVI style notes: Cards

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Cards (cards/index.html; plan 2 M9 block 3, design agreed with Mako 2026-11-10 19:17, built 2026-11-10; his phone check pending)
- Every card in one place. A front end only: cards/catalogue.json (fetch-cards.js), data/prices.json and data/rules.json, read through decks/deck.js (the name, the printings, the ban list). The page's own index.html shares the folder with the card data. Prior art, patterns only: Scryfall and Piltover Archive (search first; a card page of text, printings with prices, legality, rulings).
- The page is search first. A sticky search plate (.srow .plate, as Rules' and News'): .sf ('Search cards') with the round X; under it the view row as Collection's (.dvrow): Grid | List (.seg.dvseg, the view chips' icons; kept in vir.cards.set.v1 with the sort), then Sort and one Filters chip (.fchip, as Decks' browser: Sort shows its arrow and name; Filters counts what is on and fills bone). Sort (#psort, rows, the arrow on the chosen one, again flips it): Name, Energy, Price. Filters (#pfls, rows with their values, each one deeper to a pick-many list #pfl with Any in its foot; Show all in its foot): Set, Type, Domain, Rarity, Energy. A card matches a Set or Rarity when any of its printings does, a Domain when it has one of the picked ones.
- Each card shows once, as its base printing (deck.js rank). A search word must be in the card's name, number, tags or type; cards that match only in their text follow those. The section head reads All cards, or Results, with the count. Grid is the Decks browser's grid, three across (.ctile): the picture is the tappable part (.cpic, kind card, a battlefield turned), the name and the price of one under it. List is rows without glass, as Collection's (.crow): picture, name, what it is, the price. The next 60 join as the end nears; Back to top (RAVI_TOTOP). Nothing matching says 'No card matches.' over Show all cards. Loading blocks: a head and nine tiles.
- A card opens full screen (.crd), the bend STYLE 7 names for Decks' card browser (Mako's yes 2026-11-10), growing out of what was tapped (RAVI_GROW) and shrinking away (RAVI_SHRINK). A sticky plate (.chead .plate) holds Close (.circ.trk), the card's name (17, one line) and what it is (12); the plate wears the card's domain colours (.plate.cg2, as a deck's plate wears the deck's). Then, in this order: the picture (.mpic, 230 wide; a battlefield landscape, 320), a tap shows the full card (.spot, kind view); Riot's text in a glass card (.ctxt, .rtx), keywords as their tags (RAVI_KW_TAGS) and Riot's :rb_ symbols as Riot's glyphs (.ksy); its numbers, a glass card of rows you read (.facts .fk: Type, Domain, Energy, Power, Might, Might bonus, Tags, Rarity, Number, Artist, Constructed: Legal or Banned, in words); Printings and 7-day price moves, a sunken list you pick in (.plist .prb, the check on the picked one; the picture, Rarity, Number and Artist follow the pick): the printing's picture, its set and kind, its number, and each finish's price with its 7-day move (.fin .mv: the arrow and the percent, never a colour); Errata and rulings, from Rules: one press group whose rows open their words in place (.door.rec.rul, kind open, .rbody), the kind's word in its Rules section's colour, lit (.rty: Errata garnet, FAQ and Ruling gold), an erratum showing New text then Old text; then Add to.
- Which entries name a card: an erratum by Riot's own card list; a question or ruling when its words hold the card's whole name as written ('Ahri' alone is not 'Ahri, Alluring'; a legend also by its title).
- Add to (#padd, opening from its button): a Finish track when the picked printing has more than one; Collection, one press group of action rows (Wishlist, No binder, each binder), each showing in grey how many it holds; Decks, each deck with how many it holds, or Build a deck (No decks yet) going to Decks. A tap adds one, the count moves and a toast says where ('Added to the Wishlist'); the menu stays open. Written as Collection and Decks write their own keys (vir.coll.v1, vir.decks.v1). A deck takes a Main deck card always, a legend only while it has none, a battlefield while it has fewer than three; runes and tokens are dim. A card with no price yet dims the Collection rows.
- Ways in: Decks' card menu, Collection's card menu and the Exchange's card menu each end with Open in Cards (.tocard, a .pact link), so nothing links out. ?c=<set-number> opens that printing's card, ?n=<name> a card by name (the price feed's names are read; one RAVI cannot place becomes the search). Reached that way, the view's button is Back (the chevron) and returns to the page you came from.
- Bar: Hub, Go to, the third slot dimmed (Soon), Settings dimmed. Go to: Your cards, after Collection and Decks. Not on the hub's default seven. The footer starts with the price source line (RAVI_STAMP) and the benchmark sentence, says whose words and pictures these are, and ends with Tell us.
- Page colour: clay 164,60,51, measured (rb-tools design/colour/cards.py: the farthest colour in Body's hue family from every page colour and the players', .130 bare and .056 in the hub's glass, the palette's own closest pair); Mako's yes 2026-11-10, clay over peach 255,196,150. Icon: a card under a magnifier, drawn for RAVI on Lucide's construction, provisional until the icon pass (plan 2 M9 block 9).
