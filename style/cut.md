# RAVI style notes: Top cut

Pattern: Tool

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Top cut (cut/index.html; M15, 2026-10-27)
- The maths is cut/cut.js (shared with the tests): Swiss pairing modelled top down by points, a win 3, a draw 1, a bye a win; every finish still open, best first, each with Worst case (the other tables' last two rounds fall the worst way for you) and its Chance (.fp, a percent in the record's type: cut.js chances plays the event out many times, every other match a coin flip with one in ten drawn (one in fifty when the picked event's page says best of 1: events.json bo), a tie on points at the cut's edge settled by luck; 100% only when the worst case is In, else never past >99% or under <1%); a tie on points reads Tiebreakers. Verdicts carry a word and a mark (In with a tick, Tiebreakers with =, Out with x); the tint only helps.
- No opening line (plan 2 M8.7 B5). Your finishes says the rounds left beside its title ('1 round left') and leads with the safe line (.fsafe, 15 bone; M8.7 E1): '3-0-1 or better gets you in for certain.', 'You can draw in.' when drawing every round left is In in the worst case, else 'No finish gets you in for certain.'; a finish's second line says what it takes ('Win the last round', 'Win 2, draw 1', 'Final record'); with no top cut: 'No top cut, so no finishes to work out.' The Event menu leaves out Learn-to-Play and Open Play and names the store short (M8.7 E2, C4). How it's worked out is four rows (Worst case, Chance, Tiebreakers, How exact this is) that open their words in place (.hwb; M8.7 B3).
- The event and Your record tint their glass in the page's colour (STYLE.md section 4, named exceptions); Your finishes stays plain, its verdict pills the only colour in it.
- Three glass cards (.csec): The event (the Event row opening #pev, Players and Swiss rounds steppers, the Top cut track None to Top 16, a note), Your record (Wins, Losses, Draws steppers; After round N of R), Your finishes (head row: Finish, Worst case, Chance; rows; the rows below the first that is Out with under 1% folded into one line). Steppers are .circ buttons either side of the value; nothing is typed. A stepper pressed and held repeats, slowly then fast, as iPhone's own stepper; a tap is one step; the finishes are worked out on release.
- Live (M16): on the event's day, RAVI's server fills the players still in, the store's Swiss rounds and, with a Riot name saved, the record so far, each note saying it is from the event page; the cut stays the usual one. Server away: as below. On the day the live file's structure also sets the cut, as below (M17 block 4).
- An event (the menu, or ?ev=<id> from the calendar card's .evcut and the Tracker page's Top cut row) fills the players (its registrations on its day, its spots before), the rounds and cut, and the record from Game matches tagged to it. Rounds and cut (M17 block 4, Mako's yes 2026-10-30): the store's Swiss rounds from the event's page (data/events.json swiss); the cut from its page (cut): Swiss only opens on None, a cut on the usual size for the players (Riot gives its size only on the day); a Summoner Skirmish opens on Top 8 from 9 players (None under) whatever the page says, as stores list Skirmishes as Swiss and add the cut; nothing known, the usual rounds and cut. The note says what came from the event page ('From the event page: 4 Swiss rounds and no top cut.'); a hand-set track or stepper wins and drops the claim. The Event row names the event as the Tracker page's Event row does (kind, then the store's short name); its label stays whole, the value is trimmed only if needed, the chevron always shows. The Event menu ends with No event, and the Event row says No event when none is picked, as the Tracker page. Bar: Hub, Go to, How it's worked out (#phow, the info icon, data-fills: Worst case, Chance, Tiebreakers, How exact this is; no standing note under Your finishes, only 'Every finish below these is out.'), Settings dimmed. (M17 sweep, Mako's yes 2026-10-30)
