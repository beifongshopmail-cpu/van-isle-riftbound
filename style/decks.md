# RAVI style notes: Decks

Pattern: Tool

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Decks (decks/index.html)
- Owned (M13): its own block at the foot of the deck, under Notes, only when the Collection holds something; closed until its round chevron is tapped, as every folding section; inside, Any printing | This printing, the count by section, the missing cards, and Add missing cards to (a menu as Collection's Move menu: No binder, each binder, the Wishlist). No warning when cards are missing. (Mako 2026-10-25)
- Two screens: the list of decks, and one deck: a sticky .plate (Back, name and price, Undo; Deck, Stats, Hand), Legend beside Runes, Battlefields, then Main, Side and Considering, each with its own view and sort, and Notes. Edit (the bar's third slot, a mode) adds steppers and add tiles; the card browser is full screen; the card menu (#pcard) opens from a card. Logic and formats in decks/deck.js.
- On Decks, the view (List, Grid, Text), the size and the sort sit in one row in the plate. A section's gear overrides them for that section. (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- On a card's picture, the count is one pill centred at its foot; Edit adds minus (a bin at one copy) and plus beside it, and the pill does not move. A row keeps the stepper (Appraise's lines, Decks' List). (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- A card picture carries at most two badges, centred low, in capitals: BANNED and PROMO. (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- Undo sits in the plate: on Decks at the right of the head, on Appraise at the end of the Yours | Theirs row. It opens a list of changes, newest first, in short words ('+1 Gust'). A row undoes only that change. (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- Public decks (plan 2 M3; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-02): Deck settings' Who sees it is Private | Link | Public (saved accounts only, dim otherwise; 6 shared at most, Link counting; a note says what others see), then Share link, dim while Private. The list's count reads 'X private' and 'X of 6 shared' with a middle dot between. My decks | Public sits under the search; on Public, Legend and Domain chips take New deck and Import's row, so the plate keeps its height, and Sort is Newest or Most copied. A Public row is the deck card: the legend, the name, 'by @user', a middle dot, 'when', the domains, 'Copied n'. A shared deck opens read-only: a glass block of who shares it (picture, name, @username, Public or Link and when, copies) with Copy to my decks and Copy deck code (Copy deck code alone in a browser tab), 14 above the block under it; Edit and Undo dim; its Deck settings hold only Report this deck.
- Likes (plan 2 M9 block 2; Mako's go 2026-11-10): a like per saved account per deck, never your own, taken back by a second tap. On a shared deck's card, a compact pill at the end of the who row (.rolike, 36, type 13): a heart and Like, then the heart filled and Liked (shape and word, never colour); dim on an unsaved account, with a Save your account row at the card's foot; none on your own deck, a Tier list deck or in a browser tab. The card's line adds 'Liked n times'. A Public row shows the heart and its count over 'Copied n' (.lk; filled and bone when yours, .lkon). Sort gains Likes. My decks | Public | Liked: Liked is the Public view narrowed to the decks you like, with the same search, chips and sort; unsaved, it says 'Likes need a saved account.' over the Save your account row. Only the count is shown: never who liked a deck, never a total for a player. The heart is Lucide's (ISC), in pages.js's icon table since the icon pass (plan 2 M9 block 9): the pill's heart 18 on the icon line as Events' Directions pill, the row's 14; filled is its filled twin.
- Tags (plan 2 M4; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-02): a deck carries tags in the player's own words, set from Tags in its long-press menu (after Open) and in Deck settings (under Show), each showing the count (None when there are none). The Tags menu, one deeper: 'For <deck>', every tag in use A to Z as a pick-many list, then New tag with Add. On My decks, a chip row above the list, one per tag in use A to Z: one chosen shows only its decks and the lede counts them; again shows all. Tags are never shared with a deck.
- The deck image (plan 2 M4; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-02, after his reference picture): Export's fourth format, Image, shows the picture with Share and Save as a file. 1080 wide on a dark ground washed in the deck's two domain colours: the name and card count left and RAVI's name right; the Legend's card and name with its runes under it, beside the battlefields stacked; Main Deck and Sideboard in rows of five under centred headings, two or more copies as a bone disc at a card's foot, the Chosen Champion first and ringed in bone; Riot's notice at the foot.
- A search's filters (plan 2 M8.5; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-07): a deck's search and the card browser show Sort, then one Filters chip counting the picks that are on. Filters opens a menu of Domain, Type, Energy and Set as rows with their values in grey, each one deeper to its pick list (Back; its foot Any); Show all at the menu's foot clears every filter. While a deck's search has text, its chips take the view row's place, so the plate keeps its height.
- Sort and group (#psort) and a section's Sort by are rows (.pick), the arrow on the chosen sort, a second tap flips it, as every sort menu (plan 2 M8.7 B7).
- Stats are tiles. A tapped tile opens in place to the full row with its chart, and Open all and Close all sit above them. (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- A change updates the page in place; pictures that stay are never rebuilt (paint() in decks/index.html). (Mako's yes 2026-10-24)
- The tour's deck (plan 2 M9 block 7; Mako 2026-11-12): the Decks tour shows Deck | Stats | Hand and Edit on a deck. With a deck of the player's own it opens that one and leaves it open. With none, an example opens read-only as a Tier list deck does: the newest winning top-cut list (data/tierlists.json), named Example deck, its card reading 'An example deck' and 'Here for the tour; it goes when the tour ends', then the TopDeck.gg line; never kept on the phone or sent anywhere (Copy to my decks keeps it if the player wants it); the list of decks comes back when the tour ends.

Bends (Browse parts, STYLE.md section 5): .plate (fixed under the wordmark, the page's width less 24, at most 456); .dvseg svg (its view icons are drawn as lines).
