# RAVI style notes: The hub

Pattern: Own design

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Hub (index.html)
- The first-time setup (plan 2 M6 block 6; account.js): once, on the installed app's first hub, it opens from your picture as the intro ends (or at once with reduced motion or the intro off); never behind the install screen or for a test tool. Its Your hub step redraws the grid as it closes (RAVI_HUB_DRAW); the All pages block opens its tray from a tap on the grid, so a redrawn block still does.
- No bar. The badge (88) in .hubname, centred in .top (height max(260px, 42vh)). At rest its rim flows (.rb.flow::after, hub only, Mako 2026-11-01): a ring in the drawn rim's band coloured by a conic gradient, bright blue and bright pink opposite each other, each fading to .35 between, turning once every 8 s (@property --rf), fading in over the drawn rim at rest and after the intro; none with reduced motion. Then .low (no plate); nav#grid of up to eight blocks (the picks, then All pages); the middle blocks sit 14 up (nth-child 2 and 5). The hub never scrolls, the counter's way (html, body overflow hidden; Mako 2026-11-01: iOS opens an installed app one status bar short and stretches it, and a hub that could scroll was sometimes left drawn about 61pt low); a phone too short for it scrolls (html.scrolls, set 0.6s after load). Open-to check at the top of the file reads vir.hub.open.v1; ?hub forces the hub.
- Texture (Mako 2026-10-31): the body's background is img/hub-runes.webp at cover, top: the domain runes on Mako's own layout and tilt (16 degrees), black coins with a bevelled rim and the glyph pressed in, lit edges in the domain colour, only ever lighter than the ground, transparent between the coins so the black there is --ground exactly. .top is clear. Made by rb-tools design/hub-texture (texture.js, texture.py). Hub only.
- No plate (plan 2 M8, Mako's yes 2026-11-04, one layer of glass): .low holds the grid with no glass and no frost; the blocks float over the texture, each frosted (blur 20 saturate 160%, as .cg), All pages too. html and body never rubber-band (overscroll-behavior none). The dark frosted plate with a curved top (2026-10-31) is retired; NEXT -- refine the hub now its plate is gone.
- The glow (Mako 2026-10-31): .sheen, first in the body, adds img/hub-runes-glow.png (each symbol lit in its domain colour, black elsewhere) with plus-lighter at .25 (Mako 2026-11-01; was .17) through a mask on .sheen::before in cq units of .sheen, so the black between the coins is never lit. Modes on html[data-glow]: rings (wide band, three centres, 30 s) on the first hub of a session; later hubs pick rings 40%, waves 40% (a wave with a fainter echo from seven centres, 84 s) or twinkle 20% (ten lights over five coins each, 5 to 9 symbols lit at once). A centre or coin changes only while its light is 0. Off for reduced motion. CSS from rb-tools design/hub-texture/glow.py. [T]
- Back on the hub (Mako 2026-11-12: a tap on Hub may be a slip): reached from a page, the hub shows the round Back (pages.js BACK, .rvback) in the top left, 18 in, level with your picture; it returns to that page, whose own Back is kept. A hub opened fresh shows none, and a page the hub opens shows none.

The hub block (app.css THE HUB BLOCK since plan 2 M8.7: one copy, drawn by the hub and by Hub layout, Mako 2026-11-08)
- .blk (rendered from RAVI_PAGES into nav#grid.grid, and by hubedit.js): three per row, square, corner 20, padding 11. Colour from --cl (page rgb) and --ci (tint). Icon .bi 60 centred above the name .bn (Poppins 700 15). Link with data-k="do".
    <a class="blk glass" href="calendar/" style="--cl:255,176,32;--ci:...;" data-k="do"><span class="bi">icon</span><span class="bn">Events</span></a>
- .blk.allp: All pages, a button in plain glass (no page colour), the nine-dot icon; it opens the All pages tray (#pall, the Go to tiles for every page, App settings last), sitting where a bar's tray would.
- The hub: eight blocks, so the hub icon (the 3-3-2 grid) stays true. Seven are the viewer's picks, in the viewer's order (App settings, Hub layout; RAVI_HUB_PICKS in pages.js; plan 2 M6, Mako 2026-11-03); All pages is always the eighth and last. Default picks, in Go to's group order (Mako 2026-11-09): Events, Tracker, Appraise, Collection, Decks, Rules, Guide (App settings until 2026-10-27; it is always in All pages). A new page needs no hub slot: it is in All pages at once and can be picked. (Mako 2026-10-26) On a phone narrower than 390, block names track -.02em so Collection fits whole (Mako's yes 2026-10-25). The grid (.low, no plate) under the badge (.hubname, 88); hub footer .hubfoot (12 muted, Riot notice).

The launch intro (moved from STYLE.md section 8, 2026-11-11)
- The launch intro: once per launch, the first time the hub shows (session flag ravi.intro.v1; off with ravi.intro.off.v1). 'Rift App' in blue over 'Van Isle' in pink, in the badge's own letters at their size, comes in and is read; the small letters fade while the four capitals travel into the badge; the glass opens out from behind them, the rim draws round from the left end both ways, meeting at the right, and the glass catches the light once. About 2.6s. The letters live inside the badge, so they move with it if the top area settles; at the end the badge is rebuilt from its markup. The hub's blocks rise in while the name is read. Any tap finishes it. No other page has one.
