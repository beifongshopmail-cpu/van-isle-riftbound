- Local (plan 2 M9 block 1; Mako's yes 2026-11-10): a local store's post is its own kind in the same list, every store alike, in the palette's deep teal 28,110,128 (the colour table's teal), the word Local beside it, the megaphone as its stand-in until the Stores page brings an icon. RAVI never shows a store's words, a private group's least of all: the card is Claude's headline (it names the store) and short summary; the menu reads 'Local, <store>, <day>', RAVI's summary, the source line ('Skyhaven Games, on Facebook.') and See it on Facebook with the out-arrow, so News is the quick look at whether to go and read the post (Mako 2026-11-10 17:59).
