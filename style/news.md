News (news/index.html; plan 2 M8.7 block 1, built 2026-11-07, waiting on Mako's phone yes)
- Reads data/feed.json (fetch-feed.js: Riot's Facebook posts through Muse, word for word; Riot's own playriftbound.com articles, cleaned as the rules watcher cleans them; RAVI's summary of each, written by Claude Haiku, else Riot's first sentence). Mako 2026-11-07: everything is read without leaving the app; only a video leaves, to be watched.
- No lede (Mako 2026-11-07). A sticky search plate (.srow .plate, as Rules'): .sf with the round X, and under it one Kinds chip (.fchip; three kinds or more fold into one chip) opening #pkind, a .pick list of the kinds there are (All kinds first); a chosen kind names the chip and fills it bone. Search as Rules': every word must match, forms, starts and typos forgiven; the headline ranks first, then the summary, then Riot's words and article; matches marked (mark.hit).
- This week (when its data is there): two glass cards (.wk): the three biggest price moves over 7 days (.wkl rows you read: single cards at $5 or more, the arrow and the percent, never a colour); the top three legends (tier/tier.js), with the Tier list attached under the card's line (.wkact holding a .pact with the tier icon, as an event card's buttons sit under its line; Mako 2026-11-07).
- Posts: each its own card, a one-row press group (.doors.nc, tinted as Rules tints its sections) whose row is a record (.door.rec.ncb, kind deeper), in its kind's colour (Mako 2026-11-07: colour like Rules): Rules jade 0,190,140; Event gold 255,200,40; Product rust 220,90,30; Announcement sky 30,205,230; Market slate 134,143,162. Riot's picture on the left (.nthumb 64, corner 12); the kind (Poppins 700 12, lit .40 toward white) beside the day (12 muted); the headline (.nh, Poppins 700 15); the summary (.ns, Nunito 13 muted, up to three lines); the chevron (kind deeper). Posts newer than the last visit (vir.news.seen.v1) gather under New, the rest under Earlier (From Riot when nothing is new); no button for it.
- A post opens its menu (#ppost), titled by its headline, its glass tinted its kind's colour as a Rules article's: the kind and day (.mty), RAVI's summary, Riot's picture (.npic, 16:9, corner 14), Riot's post (plain text, no links made), Riot's article (.rtx, cleaned a second time in the page; pictures only from Riot's CDN), the source line (.rsrc); a video post adds Watch on Facebook (or YouTube) with the out-arrow, the one way out.
- The footer starts with the price source line (RAVI_STAMP) and the benchmark sentence, says whose words these are, and ends with Tell us.
- Bar: Hub, Go to, the third slot dimmed (Soon; the M8.7 sweep judges every Soon slot), Settings dimmed. Page colour Lilac 170,140,255, clear glass (its posts carry the colour, as the calendar): as built, not chosen; the M8.7 colour pass settles it. Icon: the megaphone (whatsnew) Rules' Latest wore.
