Check your email.'; Riot's Pacific time is island time, another zone's time is left out. It leaves New and Earlier while pinned. Soonest end first. - This week (when its data is there): one glass row that opens in place, as Collection's Movers (.wkbox, kind open, the chevron; Mako 2026-11-07): closed on a visit, unless the three price moves (which cards, which way) or the three legends (and tiers) differ from what this phone last showed (vir.news.week.v1), then open and marked Changed; a first visit is the baseline.
