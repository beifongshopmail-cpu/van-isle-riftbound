Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Rules (rules/index.html)
- Reads data/rules.json (fetch-rules.js copies Riot's words; the page never rewrites them). No opening line (plan 2 M8.7 B5). A search field (.sf with the round X, hidden while empty) in a sticky .plate with one Filters chip under the search (.bchips .fchip, as Decks'; plan 2 M8.7 B1): it counts what is on and opens #pfls, Kind and Set as rows with their values, each one deeper (#pfl, a pick list: Any, then Errata and FAQs, or each set oldest first; a pick returns to Filters), Show all in its foot; with no search they list what they choose, then Current documents (Riot's two PDFs as links with the out-arrow .extl; the two legality lists as deeper rows), Errata and FAQs and rulings (a .doors row per article, newest first, with its count).
- An article opens #part: Riot's warning first where it has one, as one closed row, Riot's note, that opens to Riot's words (.rnote .rnrow .rntx; plan 2 M8.7 F2), its entries grouped as Riot groups them (.lb), each a .door.rec of kind open with its body (.rbody, Riot's markup in .rtx) under it, then the source line (.rsrc). Pictures stay on Riot's page (a .rpic link). Search lists matching entries the same way, grouped by kind (Errata, then FAQs and rulings, each in its section's colour), at most 40. It forgives: every typed word matches a word, its form (conquer, conquered), a word it starts, or one a letter or two off; the card's own name ranks first, then the question, then the text. Matched words are marked (mark.hit: bold and lit, never colour alone) in the names and in an opened entry. (Mako's phone yes 2026-11-03)
- An erratum's new text marks the words it changed (mark.chg: bold and underlined, never colour alone; the longest common run of words against the old text), and its old text folds under a quiet Old text row (.rold) (plan 2 M8.7 F1).
- Bar: Hub, Go to, Updates (#tabupd, the log icon until the icon pass; data-fills #pupd), Settings dimmed. Updates (#pupd): Riot's posts about what changed that the sections do not hold (Core Rules patch notes, ban list posts, Tournament Rules changelogs: rules.json later), newest first, with their count in the head; each a record row of kind open (its title without Riot's filing tags, its day and kind under it: Core Rules, Tournament Rules, Ban list), opening in place to one line on what it is and the source line's link to the post; no colour; NEW as the entries, and leaving the page records the newest post too. NEW on an entry, a document or an article's row means changed since the last visit (vir.rules.seen.v1, the newest 'changed' day; a first visit is the baseline), as the calendar's NEW: badges stay while the page is open, leaving it records the visit. Latest retired for it (Mako 2026-10-24). The footer ends with Tell us, as Calendar and Trade.
- Keywords in Riot's text (plan 2 M8.7 F3, Mako's yes 2026-11-08; built 2026-11-09): a bracketed keyword Riot prints as a tag ([Hidden], [Deflect 2]) is drawn as that tag (app.css .nkw, pages.js RAVI_KW and RAVI_KW_TAGS), in the colour the cards print it, the word kept; and a bracketed symbol Riot's glyph set names is drawn as Riot's glyph (app.css .ksy, 16, the text's own height; Mako 2026-11-09: 20 read a tad big; pages.js RAVI_SYM: [M] and [S] Might, [E] Exhaust, [A] Power of any domain, [R] Fury Power, [0] to [8] Energy), in an entry's words and its question; [C] (1 Power of the card's own domain) and [>] (no symbol of its own) stay as Riot wrote them.
