Share my legend (#plocal): a check row (vir.tier.auto.off.v1). Hub layout (#phub; Mako 2026-11-03): a small copy of the hub's grid (.hlg: its 3-3-2 rows and arc, each block the page's coloured glass with its tinted icon at 34 and name at 12, .hlb), All pages fixed last (.hla), empty places dashed (.hle); under Not on the hub, the other pages three across (.hls, icons 24). Drag a block to move it (the others make room), drag a page from below onto a block to swap it in or onto an empty place to add it, drag a hub block below to take it off; or tap one, then another (the picked one ringed, .picked; a drop target ringed, .over; the dragged block .lift). Take off the hub in the foot, dim until a hub block is picked. The hub keeps this order (RAVI_HUB_PICKS). Open the app to (#popen): rows from RAVI_PAGES plus Hub. Motion (#pmotion): Opening animation, a check row.
