# RAVI style notes: Guide

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Guide (start/index.html)
- New to RAVI opens with What RAVI is, then Setup: one line and Set up RAVI (.pact, #nsetup: a lone action), which runs the first-time setup again (account.js RAVI_SETUP.open; plan 2 M6 block 6).
- Two guides on one track (.gsel: New to Riftbound, New to RAVI; #ravi opens the second; vir.start.guide.v1 keeps the last one).
- New to Riftbound runs: What Riftbound is, then How to play, then At the store (Try it first, Your first deck, Signing up, Kinds of events, What to bring, The rules). No opening line over the guide track (plan 2 M8.7 B5). At the store's cards keep their first lines and link row; the rest folds under More as How to play's (plan 2 M8.7 B4). Kinds of events gives each kind its calendar mark (.nkd .nkm, 12, its colour lifted .40 toward white) in the app's order (M8.7 C8). Every link to Events reads Find events (C7). New to RAVI waits for its own guided tour (Mako 2026-11-08: NEXT -- a guided tour of RAVI). How to play and At the store are labels over their cards (.lb.nprt, muted). (plan 2 M6 block 5; Mako's yes 2026-11-03)
- How to play is eight glass cards (.nsec): Your deck, The board, Setting up, A turn, Runes, Units and moving, Fights, Scoring. Each is a section head, a summary of one or two sentences, one picture (.nfig), then the detail under More: a quiet row under the card's divider (.nfo, data-k open, 44 high, Poppins 600 15, the chevron turning; the line is the event card's 1px at .07), opening .nmore in place with .reveal. Setting up's More holds only its rules.
- Words: 'hold' is only the scoring word (holding a battlefield as your turn starts); a battlefield someone has is one they control (Mako 2026-11-03). Riot's quoted words stay as written.
- Steps are a list (.nst) with a 34 by 26 marker (.nmk, --track, Poppins 700 12): numbers, or A, B, C, D, Act and End for a turn.
- Pictures are drawn in Kit tokens and words: every zone carries its name, and yours and theirs are written (.nsl), never shown by colour alone (yours --p1t with --p1r lines, theirs --p2t with --p2r, .nzy and .nzt). Corners inside a picture are page-local (5 to 12).
- The board is Riot's official mat (Mako 2026-11-03: his preferred layout, without the score track: points are kept on paper, dice, a dial or an app): Battlefield Zone, Legend Zone, Champion Zone; Base: Units + Gears, Main Deck; Rune Deck, Base: Runes, Trash, with the mat's own zone names (.nbd, a grid; zones .nz at 12, Poppins 600). Every name whole at 360.
- Card pictures are Riot's own, from cards/art (.ncd, .nrj, .nrr): Jinx, Loose Cannon; Jinx, Demolitionist; a Fury Rune; Grove of the God-Willow, every card one size (Mako 2026-11-03): the battlefield, a card on its side, as wide as the others are tall (.nland), stacked three deep, each 20 behind the one above (Mako 2026-11-03), with the two behind face down (.nlay; Riot's black back, turned); the Main deck a card's size, as Riot's blue back. Captions: Legend, 1; Chosen Champion, 1; Runes, 12; Battlefields, 3; Main deck, 40 (39 + Chosen Champion). The card backs (cards/backs: black for battlefields and legends, white for runes, blue for every other card) came from Mako, tuned to one size, frame and corner (2026-11-03). Domain symbols are Riot's rune files from cards/runes, unchanged: inline beside a Power cost (.nsy, 20) and the six domains under Runes' More (.ndom, 28). Card-text symbols are Riot's glyphs in cards/glyphs (Energy in a circle, the rainbow any Power, Might, Exhaust), brought unchanged by fetch-glyphs.js (its report, glyphs.md, gives each file's size), inline beside their words (.nsy). Riot's set has no keyword icons (2026-11-03).
- Units and moving is one grid (.nmv): two real battlefields (.nmc: Grove of the God-Willow, Monastery of Hirana) with Ganking only between them, an arrow under the middle of each, then Your base across (.nmb) holding a real unit (.nmu: Flame Chompers). Fights uses real cards (.nu): their Kinkou Monk (4 Might), your Flame Chompers (3) and Mystic Poro (2), each card's fate written under it.
- The board's Legend Zone, Champion Zone, Main Deck, Rune Deck and Trash are card-shaped: 72 wide columns, rows a card's height (Mako 2026-11-03). A card whose picture carries Riot's BANNED stamp is never used. An exhausted rune is the rune card turned 90 degrees (.nx).
- Facts come from Riot's own documents. Every More ends with the rules its card rests on (.nqs): a head naming Riot's Core Rules and their date (.nqh, 13 muted), each rule's number and Riot's words word for word (.sel, 13), then a Rules row to the Rules page (Mako 2026-11-03). rb-tools p2m6h H10 checks each quote against Riot's text and fails when Riot dates a newer Core Rules.

- How to play's eight cards each wear a palette colour as coloured glass, walking the hues: Your deck Garnet, The board Tangerine, Setting up Gold, A turn Lime, Runes Mint, Units and moving Sky, Fights Tanzanite, Scoring Orchid (plan 2 M8; Mako 2026-11-03). Keywords in the guide's own words are drawn as the cards print them (.nkw): a slanted tag in Riot's colour (sampled from the card images: teal --kwt 14,116,94 for Accelerate, Action, Reaction; crimson --kwr 204,44,104 for Tank, Assault; lime --kwg 146,176,44 for Ganking, with ink text), the word in capitals, Poppins 700 12. Riot's quoted rules keep their own text.

- New to RAVI's page list has every page but itself and App settings, News after Rules (plan 2 M8.7 A3, Mako's yes 2026-11-10).
