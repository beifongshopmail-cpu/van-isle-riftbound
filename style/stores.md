# RAVI style notes: Stores

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Stores (stores/index.html; plan 2 M9 block 4, design agreed with Mako 2026-11-11 08:50, built that day; his phone check pending)
- Every store on the island that runs Riftbound, each alike (STYLE 1, store neutrality): no store first by anything but the alphabet, none marked, every row the same shape. A front end only: data/stores.json (fetch-events.js buildStores: every store that has had an event on RAVI, kept 120 days after its last one), data/events.json (its coming events), data/feed.json (its posts, RAVI's summary only, as News), data/store-info.json (hours, phone and website by store id, kept by hand from each store's own listing with the day checked; a store with no entry shows none of them). Prior art, patterns only: Wizards' store locator (a store's address, phone, website and coming events).
- The list: towns A to Z, each a section head (the town, its count) over one press group (.doors) of its stores A to Z. A store's row (.door.rec, kind deeper, the chevron): its name, and under it (.snx, 12 muted, one line) whether it is open, in words, once its hours are known ('Open, closes 8 PM', 'Closed, opens Thu 11 AM'), then its next event as its type and when ('Nexus Nights, Wed Nov 18, 6:00 PM'), or 'No events listed'. Loading blocks: two heads and their rows.
- Areas: the Events page's own saved choice (vir.cal.filters.v1, towns: the ones off; the key's other filters are carried untouched), so an area is set once for both pages. Stores settings (#pset, the bar's Settings) is one list, Areas: each town with stores as a pick-many row with its count and check (.tyrow, as Events'). With every area off: 'No stores in your areas.' over Show every area.
- A store opens as a menu (#pstore), as a card does on Cards: the head is its name and Close. The body, in this order: Where, the street address (.sadr, .sel; the town alone when the feed has none) over Directions (.pact, the out-arrow; the phone's own maps, as Events); Hours, when known: the state in words (.snow), a glass card of the week from today (.hrs .hk, today first and bold, 'Closed' or '11 AM to 8 PM'), and 'Hours checked <day>. Holidays can differ.'; Contact, when known: Call and Website as action rows; Next events, a press group of up to six (the type, then when), each going to Events on that day (../calendar/?day=), then '<n> more on Events.', or 'No events listed.'; Lately, when the store has posts News would still show: up to three rows that open RAVI's summary in place (kind open), never the store's words. The foot is Follow its events, the store's own calendar feed (data/store/<id>.ics, as Events' Follow).
- Ways in: an opened event card on Events starts with the store's name as a compact pill (.evstore, calendar) to ../stores/?s=<id>; a Local post's menu on News gains Store details (.nstore) when its source names one store. ?s=<id> opens that store's menu over the list, even when its area is off.
- Bar: Hub, Go to, the third slot dimmed (Soon), Settings (Stores settings). Go to: Playing, straight after Events. Not on the hub's default seven. The footer says how stores are listed and ends with Tell us.
- Page colour: plum 151,97,125, measured (the farthest colour in Events' and Tracker's hue family from every page colour, the players' and Local's teal: .135 bare, .059 in the hub's glass); a trial until Mako's yes. Icon: Lucide's store as drawn, filled with the door a hole; provisional until the icon pass (plan 2 M9 block 9).
