# RAVI style notes: Stores

Pattern: Browse (reference page: Cards)

Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Stores (stores/index.html; plan 2 M9 block 4, design agreed with Mako 2026-11-11 08:50, built that day; his phone check pending)
- Every store on the island that runs Riftbound, each alike (STYLE 1, store neutrality): no store first by anything but the alphabet, none marked, every row the same shape. A front end only: data/stores.json (fetch-events.js buildStores: every store that has had an event on RAVI, kept 120 days after its last one), data/events.json (its coming events), data/feed.json (its posts, RAVI's summary only, as News), data/store-info.json (hours, phone and website by store id, kept by hand from each store's own listing with the day checked; a store with no entry shows none of them). Prior art, patterns only: Wizards' store locator (a store's address, phone, website and coming events).
- The plate (as News'): a sticky search plate (.srow .plate): .sf 'Search stores' with the round X (every word must start a word of the name, town or street; 'No store matches.' when none does), and under it the view row (.dvrow) with Grid | List (.seg.dvseg, the view chips' icons; vir.stores.view.v1, Grid first).
- The list: the Browse pattern as Cards builds it. The list's head (.shead #lhead) reads All stores, or Results while searching, with the count. By town A to Z, then name; no town heads, the town is on the card. Each store its own card, a one-row press group (.doors.stc) in plain glass, every store the same shape (store neutrality); no colour on a store (a store has no kind: STYLE.md section 4, the ladder). In Grid (.sgrid, two across, gap 8) a card is a tile (.stg, a glass button): its photo on top (.sic, 2:1, corner 14: img/stores/<sid>.webp, 960 x 480, cropped from Mako's pictures, listed in PICS; a store with none keeps the stand-in, the store icon in muted on --recess), the name (.snm, Poppins 700 15, three lines at most), the town in grey (.stw b, Poppins 700 12) with whether it is open in muted words beside it once its hours are known ('Open, closes 8 PM', 'Closed, opens Thu 11 AM') and the chevron; then, under a line across the card (.snext, the card's second section), 'Next:' and when in grey (.swn), over its next event's type with Events' mark and hue (.sev, .mk, the type lit .40 as on Events' cards: the one colour on the card, since it names a kind; never colour alone, the name beside it; a type's name is never cut, so it keeps a line of its own), or 'No events listed'. In List a card is a record row (.door.rec.stl) in the same grey as a Grid tile (the flat fill, --flat, on its group's glass; the group's press shade), with its 64 photo or stand-in on the left (.sic, corner 12), the same lines in a column (.stt, no line across; the type keeps its own line: after its date it fits 4 stores of 12 at 402, none at 360) and the chevron. No match: 'No store matches.' over Show all stores, which clears the search. Loading blocks: a head, then this phone's view (ntiles, or cards with a picture).
- Areas: the Events page's own saved choice (vir.cal.filters.v1, towns: the ones off; the key's other filters are carried untouched), so an area is set once for both pages. Stores settings (#pset, the bar's Settings) is one list, Areas: each town with stores as a pick-many row with its count and check (.tyrow, as Events'). With every area off: 'No stores in your areas.' over Show every area.
- A store opens as a menu (#pstore), as a card does on Cards: the head is its name and Close. The menu is plain glass. The body, in this order: its photo (.smpic, 2:1, corner 14) when it has one; its town in grey (.mtw); no labels over the address, hours or contact (each says what it is): the street address (.sadr, .sel; the town alone when the feed has none) over Directions (.pact, the out-arrow; the phone's own maps, as Events); the hours when known, words on the menu with no surface (glass means press): the state in bold (.snow), the week from today a day a line (.hrs .hk, 15, muted; today first, bone and bold in the same face; 'Closed' or '11 AM to 8 PM'), and 'Hours checked <day>. Holidays can differ.'; Call and Website as action rows when known; Next events, a press group of up to six (the type with its mark, then when), each going to Events on that day (../calendar/?day=), then '<n> more on Events.', or 'No events listed.'; Lately, when the store has posts News would still show: up to three rows that open RAVI's summary in place (kind open), never the store's words. The foot is Follow their events, the store's own calendar feed (data/store/<id>.ics, as Events' Follow).
- Ways in: an opened event card on Events starts with the store's name as a compact pill (.evstore, calendar) to ../stores/?s=<id>; a Local post's menu on News gains Store details (.nstore) when its source names one store. ?s=<id> opens that store's menu over the list, even when its area is off.
- Bar: Hub, Go to, the third slot dimmed (Soon), Settings (Stores settings). Go to: Playing, straight after Events. Not on the hub's default seven. The footer says how stores are listed and ends with Tell us.
- Page colour: plum 151,97,125, on the bar's pill, its Go to icon and its hub block only (the ladder, step 1); (open) until Mako sees it there. Icon: Lucide's store as drawn, filled with the door a hole; provisional until the icon pass (plan 2 M9 block 9).
