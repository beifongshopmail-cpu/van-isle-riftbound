Read with STYLE.md (the core) before touching this page. Lines moved verbatim from STYLE.md sections 5 and 10 on 2026-11-03. A class this page defines that app.css does not is listed under Page-local classes (rb-tools style-lint checks it).

Tier list (tier/index.html; M17, 2026-10-28)
- The maths is tier/tier.js (shared with the fetcher and the tests); the data is data/tier.json, written daily by fetch-tier.js from TopDeck.gg: each event's date, size, cut and placings with Riot's legend names only, never a player. TopDeck is credited in the footer.
- Tier colours (Mako 2026-11-09: the pastels read cheap as glass): the familiar ladder at the palette's strength, S fury red, A body orange, B order yellow, C calm green, Rogue mind blue (app.css WHERE COLOUR GOES, --h-*); the letter block solid with ink, the row coloured glass in it. As built, not chosen.
- Five rows always, S, A, B, C, Rogue, each named in words; an empty tier keeps its row, slim (its block 48 high, a tile), says No legend here right now and dims its block (.4, no tap, as STYLE 1; Mako 2026-11-10). Each row is a shelf of whole legend cards, 68 wide with no name under them (the card shows it, the label says it; Mako 2026-11-09), never cropped, scrolling sideways card by card with a fade at an edge that has more. The letter block (the letter alone, as any tier list draws it; the count is in its label; plan 2 M8.7) opens the whole tier, best conversion first; a card opens its legend, laid out as Decks' card menu: Name, Title as the menu's title, the whole card centred 150 wide, then its tier, one sentence, Played, Top cut, Conversion (Mako 2026-11-09), one deeper from a tier's menu. Search above the board: while searching, a tier with no match folds away, and a search that matches nothing shows one line, No legend matches. (#tqnone, a .tempty; plan 2 M8.7 A2, Mako's yes 2026-11-10; before, each such tier said No match). No second view of the board. (Mako's yes 2026-10-29)
- Global | Local (Mako 2026-10-29): a track under the search, remembered. Local is the viewer's Events areas (the areas left on in Events settings; with none off, Vancouver Island; some off, 'your areas'; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-10); its decks are added by players on the honour system: the legend most picks name stands, a tie says Contested and is left out. Counts only (whole cards, 'n decks', most played first) until 100 decks, then the same five shelves.
- Add decks: a full-width button under Local's line. Each menu goes one deeper: finished events from the last 14 days, an event's final standings (placing, Riot name, record, legend), a legend picker (search, whole cards, your pick ringed in bone, Take back my pick). A Riot name is written as the Live menu writes it (the tag 12 muted, You 12 bone on mine) and a record as wins-losses-draws, all three (3-0-0). (M17 sweep, Mako's yes 2026-10-30)
- Bar: Hub, Go to, How it's counted (#phow, the info icon, data-fills), Settings dimmed. How it's counted is six rows (The data, Played and Top cut, Conversion, Tiers, Local, Lists and cards) that open their words in place (.hwb), then 'Legends and decks only' as its note (plan 2 M8.7 B3).
- No opening line; the search and Global | Local sit in a sticky plate (.srow .plate, as News'; plan 2 M8.7 B2, B5).
- A legend's lists (plan 2 M4; Mako's phone yes 2026-11-02): Global only, under its numbers, three groups each under a .lb label: Top-cut lists (rows, best placing first, then newest: '1st of 272' over 'Oct 31', a middle dot, 'opens in Decks'; five, then All n lists in place), Most played cards (a small whole card, Riot's name, 'In x of n lists, y copies', the share right; eight, then All n cards), Public decks (one row to that legend's most copied on Decks). A line names TopDeck.gg and that names are never kept. A list opens read-only on Decks with no person: where it placed, 'A top-cut list from TopDeck.gg', Copy to my decks and Copy deck code, no Report.
