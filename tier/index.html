How it's counted

Every morning RAVI reads the last 30 days of Riftbound Constructed events of 16 players or more from TopDeck.gg, and keeps only each finish's legend. Player names are never kept.

Played: a legend's share of every deck. Top cut: its share of every top-cut spot (the cut the event played; an event that stopped after Swiss counts a top 8 from 17 players, a top 4 below).

Conversion: top cut divided by played. 1.00x is average; 2.00x makes the top cut twice as often as its play share predicts. A legend with few decks is pulled toward 1.00x, so a lucky handful cannot top the list.

S: 10% of decks or more and 1.20x or better. A: 6% and 1.05x. B: 4% and 0.90x. C: 1% and 0.70x. R, Rogue: everything else.

Local: the decks players add for finished events in the areas you have on in Events settings. Placings come from Riot's final standings; each placing's legend is the player's own pick when they add it, else the one most picks name. Picks from saved accounts whose past picks matched the players' own count a little more, and a placing two legends tie on is left out until another pick settles it. Counts only until 100 decks are in, then tiers, counted the same way.

Top-cut lists and Most played cards: the same events' decklists, read from TopDeck.gg. A legend's menu shows its top-cut lists, best placing first, and the cards most of its lists run. A list is kept without its player's name.

Legends and decks only: RAVI never ranks players.
