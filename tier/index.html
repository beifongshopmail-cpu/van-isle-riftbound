How it's counted

Played: a legend's share of every deck. Top cut: its share of every top-cut finish (the cut the event played; after Swiss only, a top 8 from 17 players and a top 4 below). Conversion: top cut over played, so 1.00x is average and higher means it reaches the cut more often than its numbers explain. Legends with few decks are pulled toward 1.00x so a lucky handful cannot top the list.

S: 10% of decks or more and 1.20x or better. A: 6% and 1.05x. B: 4% and 0.90x. C: 1% and 0.70x. Rogue: the rest. Legends and decks only: RAVI never ranks players.
